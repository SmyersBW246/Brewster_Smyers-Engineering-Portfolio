## Current design work

The provisional flat X-frame is 102 × 102 mm with a 3 mm thickness. I am working through motor and electronics packaging, insulated mounting interfaces, mold-friendly geometry and a matched-specimen comparison plan.

Commercial flight-control electronics keep the project focused on the mechanical structure. The laboratory process, mold envelope and purchased-component fit still require confirmation.

## Planned comparison

I plan to compare mass, load-deflection behavior, vibration, fastener integrity and manufacturing effort using matched geometry and shared electronics. Composite superiority is a hypothesis to test.

Next steps are parametric CAD and fit checks, process coupons and comparison frames. Fabrication, structural tests and flight demonstrations have not yet occurred.

## Current documentation
