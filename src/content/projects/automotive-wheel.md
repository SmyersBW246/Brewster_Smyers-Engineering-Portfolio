## Design focus

The current work focuses on the rim cross-section, hub mounting pad, center interface and lug geometry before refining the spokes. I am interested in how those interfaces constrain automotive packaging and how the spokes provide load paths between the rim and hub.

## Next development steps

I plan to reconcile dimensions with verified vehicle and wheel-interface data, complete the model and define mechanical load cases. Static, fatigue and impact analyses are future work, followed by a review of manufacturability.

Mass, stiffness and stress comparisons will be reported only when supported by documented analysis. The concept has not passed structural or fitment validation and is not presented as a road-ready component.
