## Mechanical design question

The early study focuses on the interface between a lightweight plate and the purchased insert, followed by bending, vibration, retention and clamp-slip testing. Generous transitions and a flat molded part simplify the initial manufacturing problem.

## Planned development

I plan to compare the molded plate with a matched printed version, first in a rigid fixture and then as an assembled mount. Measuring plate flexure separately from clamp movement will help identify which component controls the response.

Final fit, manufacturing, fatigue behavior and retention durability remain to be demonstrated.

## Current evidence

The drawing is a preliminary reference sketch from the design study. It explains the proposed layout rather than documenting a manufactured part. The molded-carbon comparison and the mount’s relationship to a class assignment remain unconfirmed.
