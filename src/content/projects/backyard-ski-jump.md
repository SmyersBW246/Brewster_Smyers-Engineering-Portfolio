## Build and iteration

I assembled the timber support frame, slatted ramp and surface panels independently in approximately two days. After trying the initial setup, I adjusted the jump angle, approach and access.

The construction photographs show the frame and finished feature; a video still records it in use. The project predates my formal engineering education and does not include instrumented testing or structural analysis.

## What it taught me

The project introduced a process I still enjoy: build something, observe what happens and make a practical improvement. It also gave me experience planning a small construction project and translating a rough concept into assembled parts.
