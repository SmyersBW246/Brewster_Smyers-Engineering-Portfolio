## Problem

I wanted my desk-rail typing angle in something I could carry. The stand had to fold flat, set the tilt without tools and hold the laptop lid slightly open so it runs with the screen on.

## My role

This is a personal project. I set the requirements, worked through the design, printed it, assembled it and tested the fit.

## What I built

**Version 1** was three nested frames: a base, a tilt frame hinged at the front, and a prop whose foot sits in one of three spots. A small flip-up paddle held the lid about 15 mm open. The parts relied on printed clips.

**Version 2** keeps the idea and changes how it is held together.

- **No clips.** Screws in heat-set inserts hold every joint, so the fit is something I can tighten and adjust.
- **Four tilts.** The prop drops into one of four grooves, designed for 6.8°, 11.8°, 15.8° and 20°. The 11.8° setting matches my desk-rail angle.
- **A latch.** A turn latch at the back keeps the stand shut when it is folded.

## Evidence

- **Version 1 failed in use.** I printed it on November 8 and tried it with my laptop. It didn't stay flat, and the lid-stop paddle would not stay clipped into the base. The printed clips could not hold a tight enough fit.
- **Version 2 is printed and assembled.** The photos at the top of this page show it folded flat and raised on its prop.

The tilt angles above are design values from the CAD. I have not measured them on the printed stand yet.

## Status

Version 2 is printed. Next I will measure the real tilt angles and the lid gap, then see how it holds up with daily use.
