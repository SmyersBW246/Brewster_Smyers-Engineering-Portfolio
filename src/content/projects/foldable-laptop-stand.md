## Summary

I wanted my desk-rail typing angle in something I could carry. The stand is three nested frames: a base, a tilt frame hinged at the front, and a prop whose round foot sits in one of three spots to give 8.8°, 11.8° or 14.8°. A small flip-up paddle holds the lid about 15 mm open so the laptop runs with the screen on. Everything folds into one 18.8 mm plate and prints in PETG with no supports. Four shoulder screws in heat-set inserts make the hinges, so there is no glue.

## Key numbers

| | Value |
|---|---|
| Laptop | Surface Laptop 6, 15 in (340 × 244 × 16.9 mm) |
| Tilt settings | 8.8°, 11.8° (my desk-rail angle) and 14.8° |
| Folded size | 307.6 × 302.5 × 18.8 mm |
| Lid gap | About 15 mm at the front edge |
| Parts | Base, tilt frame, prop and lid-stop paddle, plus 4 screws and 4 inserts |

## Where it stands

I printed the first version on November 8. The lid-stop paddle would not stay clipped into the base. I am now working on version 2, which fixes the paddle's fit. After that I will check the real tilt angles and how well it holds up with daily use.
